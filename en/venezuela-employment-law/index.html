Where we begin

Employment risk often starts well before a dispute

Hiring terms, remuneration practices, employee records, time-off management and internal procedures can have significant legal and financial consequences. Reviewing them early gives businesses more options.

Our specialized legal team examines the facts, documentation and applicable provisions of Venezuela’s Organic Labor Law (LOTTT), always in the context of how the business actually operates.

Employment counsel throughout the workforce lifecycle

  • Hiring and documentation
  • Compensation and obligations

Hiring and documentation

Employment agreements, policies, role descriptions, personnel records and onboarding structures.

Compensation and obligations

Review of remuneration arrangements, benefits, classification of payments and formal compliance duties.

Terminations and reorganization

Assessment of separation scenarios, negotiations, contingent exposure and documented decisions.

Inspections and disputes

Preparation for administrative proceedings, individual or collective claims and coordinated defense.

A template cannot determine the legal consequences of a real employment relationship.

The characterization of an employment arrangement, a payment or a termination depends on facts, evidence and applicable law. We favor documented, defensible decisions over standardized answers.

What to prepare for the initial review

  • Contract, job description and actual working arrangements
  • Pay records, compensation policies and a timeline of the decision
  • Any notices or requests from the Labor Inspectorate

Frequently asked questions

Can you review employment agreements before hiring?

Yes. We review the actual role, working conditions and proposed documents before the relationship begins.

Do you handle employee terminations?

Yes. The legal approach depends on the reason, seniority, status and applicable rules; a single model does not fit every case.

Can you support labor inspections?

Yes. We help organize records and plan a response to administrative inquiries and proceedings.

Do you support foreign businesses employing people in Venezuela?

Yes. We align employment considerations with the corporate structure and local operating model.
