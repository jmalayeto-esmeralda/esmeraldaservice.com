Frequently asked questions

What a company usually asks before entering Venezuela.

What does Esmeralda Corporate Services include?

It includes company formation, corporate administration, local governance, accounting and tax coordination, banking support and operational assistance.

Is the recommended structure usually a C.A. or S.R.L.?

It depends on the case, but for commercial operations requiring flexibility we usually analyze the C.A. as the main alternative to the S.R.L.

Do you assist foreign companies?

Yes. The service is designed for foreign companies, e-commerce, holdings, consumer brands and regional groups needing local presence.

Do you offer local director services?

We can structure local officer services with limited authority, supporting documentation, written instructions and compliance review.

Does it include accounting and taxes?

It includes accounting and tax coordination with specialized professionals, as well as follow-up on formal duties and periodic reporting.

Do you promise bank account approval?

We do not promise bank approval. We support the process, documentation, coordination and control structure required for the banking relationship.
