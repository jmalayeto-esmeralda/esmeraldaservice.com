Structure and tax

Tax exposure belongs in the business plan, not only in the return

Contracts, invoicing, legal entities, cross-border payments and local activities may produce different tax consequences. Early review helps document decisions and avoid avoidable exposure.

Our legal analysis is coordinated with the accounting information needed for effective decision-making and compliance.

Planning and structuring

Assessment of tax consequences in transactions, reorganizations and local presence structures.

Ongoing tax compliance

Audits and disputes

Administrative requests, assessments and defense strategy before SENIAT and municipal tax authorities.

Local and payroll-related taxes

Assessment of municipal charges and activity-linked contributions as applicable.

Tax rates, exemptions and filing duties should not be generalized.

The applicable treatment depends on the facts, taxpayer, transaction date and governing rules. Every conclusion must be supported by a case-specific assessment.
