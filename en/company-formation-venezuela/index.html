Foreign shareholders

International groups need careful local coordination.

Foreign individuals and companies may participate in Venezuelan corporate structures, but the incorporation process should be coordinated carefully where shareholders, directors or corporate documents originate outside Venezuela.

We assist with the review and preparation of foreign corporate documentation, powers of attorney, legalization or apostille requirements and the local documentation needed to complete the incorporation process.

Where the Venezuelan entity forms part of a broader international group, we also consider governance, authority, reporting lines and operational control from the outset.

More than incorporation

The company also needs an operating framework.

Registering the company is only the first step. A Venezuelan entity also needs an operational framework capable of supporting contracts, banking, accounting, tax compliance, employment, permits and relationships with local counterparties.

For that reason, our company formation work can be integrated with Esmeralda's Corporate Services practice, including ongoing corporate administration, local governance, tax and accounting coordination, banking support and compliance.

Market entry route

Company formation should fit the entry strategy.

Not every foreign company entering Venezuela should begin with incorporation. Depending on the proposed activity, transaction, timeline and level of local presence, the appropriate route may require preliminary due diligence, contractual arrangements, local partnerships or a broader market-entry assessment before establishing a permanent entity.

Esmeralda helps clients evaluate these considerations before committing to a structure.
