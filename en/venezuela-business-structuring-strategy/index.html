Define the commercial objective before committing capital

For an acquisition, partnership or expansion, we review how capital will be committed, who controls key decisions and what needs to be verified before signing. The legal vehicle, counterparty agreements and exit provisions follow from those choices.

Esmeralda coordinates the contractual, corporate, tax and regulatory work so those decisions carry through to documents, approvals and execution.

Frequently asked questions

Must I incorporate a Venezuelan company to do business there?

Not necessarily. It depends on the activity, duration, exposure and required local presence.

Can you structure a venture with a local partner?

Yes. We assess governance, contributions, decision rights, responsibility and exit terms.

Do you provide legal due diligence?

Yes. Due diligence may be a preliminary phase of an investment or acquisition.

Can you help before an investor commits to the market?

Yes. Early-stage planning is often the best time to compare the legal options.
