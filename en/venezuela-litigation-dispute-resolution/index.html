Dispute strategy

Before litigating, define what a successful outcome means

A conflict may call for negotiation, urgent protective measures, arbitration, litigation or a combination of steps. The right path requires assessment of cost, timing, evidence, enforcement and ongoing commercial relationships.

Esmeralda coordinates the specialized legal work under a single case strategy and a consistent client relationship.

Frequently asked questions

Does every commercial dispute require a lawsuit?

No. Negotiation and alternative resolution should be considered where appropriate.

Can you advise in shareholder disputes?

Yes. We review governing documents, corporate decisions and the relevant facts.

Do you advise on protective court measures?

We assess availability and evidentiary requirements in the specific case.

Can you assess an arbitration clause?

Yes. We review the clause’s scope and practical effect before recommending a route.
