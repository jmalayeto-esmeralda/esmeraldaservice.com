Identifying the heirs is only the beginning of an inheritance matter

The estate must be identified, ownership records and liabilities reviewed, succession duties assessed and a workable distribution of assets developed. Where heirs disagree, a documented strategy may create options before protracted court proceedings become necessary.

Esmeralda’s specialized legal team approaches inheritance matters through both succession law and the economic reality of the family assets. We assess consensual or judicial partition based on the evidence and available agreement.

Frequently asked questions

Can heirs agree on how to distribute an estate?

In appropriate circumstances, yes. A negotiated partition requires review of heir status, legal entitlements, estate assets and required formalities.

Can one heir receive a particular property while another receives compensation?

A tailored allocation and compensation arrangement may be possible, subject to ownership, valuations, inheritance rights and legally effective documentation.

Do you handle estates with real estate and business holdings?

Yes. We assess company shares, land and buildings, registries and related obligations within a coordinated succession strategy.

Are inheritance tax filings and estate partition the same thing?

No. A tax declaration addresses fiscal obligations; partition and adjudication address legal distribution and ownership.

Can heirs living outside Venezuela appoint representatives?

We assess powers of attorney, foreign documents and applicable formalities for heirs abroad.

What happens if the heirs cannot agree?

We review negotiation options and, when warranted, judicial partition and other means of protecting estate rights.
