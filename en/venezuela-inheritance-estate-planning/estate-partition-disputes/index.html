Estate Partition and Inheritance Disputes in Venezuela

We advise heirs on disagreements involving real estate, company interests and other inherited assets. We assess whether a distribution can be negotiated, what it would take to implement it, and which judicial options are available if an agreement cannot be reached.

The legal decision

Identify what the heirs can legally allocate before negotiating distribution

An inventory and appraisals help compare proposals. Their feasibility also depends on ownership, encumbrances and each party’s legal rights. A succession tax declaration addresses fiscal obligations. Partition and allocation require documentation of their own.

If an asset cannot be physically divided or valuations differ, we assess options such as allocating it to one heir with an equalization payment, selling it where appropriate, or using another estate asset to balance the agreement.

How we can help

  • Estate records and inventory
  • Appraisals and distribution proposals
  • Negotiated partition and implementation
  • Judicial partition and asset preservation

Estate records and inventory

We review title, shares of the estate, liabilities and the status of tax filings. We identify missing powers of attorney and registry documents.

Appraisals and distribution proposals

We compare appraisal dates, assumptions and scope. We model allocations and payments so the parties can assess proposals on a consistent basis.

Negotiated partition and implementation

We negotiate and document the proposed allocation, conditions, transfers and payments. We check who must sign, whether court approval is needed and how to formalize the agreement.

Judicial partition and asset preservation

We assess the forum, evidence, possible measures to preserve assets and litigation costs. We align preparation for court with the negotiation strategy.

What to prepare for the initial review

  • Information about the deceased and the heirs
  • Estate inventory, title records and available appraisals
  • Available succession tax documents and filings
  • Prior proposals and any pending proceedings or notices

Frequently asked questions

Can one heir receive an entire property?

That may be possible depending on title, the heirs’ shares, the circumstances of other heirs and applicable formalities. We review how any equalization payment would work and be documented.

Can the matter move forward if an heir lives abroad?

We can review representation, powers of attorney and formalities for foreign documents before deciding on the appropriate process.
