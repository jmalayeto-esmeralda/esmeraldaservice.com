Succession Planning for Venezuelan Assets

We help families and business owners plan the transfer of Venezuelan assets. The work starts with ownership records, family circumstances and how the assets and businesses are actually managed.

Discuss succession planningExplore services
The legal decision

Align wills, ownership and business continuity

Property may be owned individually, jointly or through a company. Before planning a transfer, we identify the interest that actually forms part of the estate and the restrictions or formalities attached to it.

In a family business, the people who benefit from shares and the people who manage operations may have different roles. We review governance, authority and company records alongside succession options and applicable reserved heirship rights.

How we can help

  • Asset inventory and title review
  • Wills and reserved inheritance rights
  • Family business continuity
  • Assets and relatives across borders

Asset inventory and title review

We organize real estate, company interests, receivables and liabilities. We identify gaps between the title records and the family’s understanding of ownership.

Wills and reserved inheritance rights

We assess your objectives, beneficiaries, any reserved shares and applicable formalities, then consider how a will would work alongside existing instruments.

Family business continuity

We review share transfer rules, management and approval rights. We propose governance changes that support the family’s succession objectives.

Assets and relatives across borders

We identify where assets are located and which local documents or reviews are needed to coordinate the plan.

What to prepare for the initial review

  • Preliminary asset inventory and locations
  • Title documents, bylaws and shareholder records where available
  • Existing wills, powers of attorney and relevant family arrangements
  • Your priorities for continuity, management and distribution

Frequently asked questions

Can a will dispose of an entire estate freely?

The scope depends in part on title and the rights of any protected heirs. We review those limits before proposing an instrument.

When should an existing plan be reviewed?

Family changes, significant acquisitions, corporate reorganizations and a move to another jurisdiction are useful points to review the plan.
