Protecting value

A trademark is more than a business name

Brand identity, creative works, software and contractual rights may shape the success of expansion, licensing or acquisition strategies. Protection is strongest when considered before launch.

Our specialized legal team identifies the assets that require filings, the rights that need contractual clarification and the ownership issues that could lead to disputes.

Filing a trademark does not automatically settle every right of use.

Availability, classes, territorial scope, ownership and potential objections must be assessed separately. A filing does not guarantee registration.

Frequently asked questions

Can you help register a trademark in Venezuela?

Yes. We assess the proposed sign, relevant classes and background before filing.

Can a trademark be licensed to another company?

Yes. Licensing requires clear contractual terms and any applicable registration formalities.

Do you advise on software and digital content?

Yes. We review ownership, development agreements and licensing models.

What if another company is using my brand?

The response depends on your existing rights, evidence and available administrative or court remedies.
