Customs Brokerage Coordination and Legal Support

We help importers appoint and work with a customs broker or agency in Venezuela. We review who can issue instructions, what supports the declaration and how questions from customs will be handled during clearance.

The legal decision

Who can act, under what authority, and on whose instructions?

Venezuela’s Organic Customs Law regulates authorized customs brokers and agencies. Esmeralda reviews the appointment and coordinates the legal documents. The authorized broker or agency handles the customs acts reserved to it by law.

When a distributor, overseas supplier, carrier and broker are involved in the same shipment, confirm who is named as consignee and who can approve corrections, costs and decisions affecting the cargo.

How we can help

  • Appointment and authority
  • Scope and brokerage charges
  • Instructions and document review
  • Clearance issues

Appointment and authority

We review the importer’s identity, powers of attorney, the agency’s authorization and the scope of the clearance engagement.

Scope and brokerage charges

We review the quote to distinguish brokerage fees, taxes, terminal charges and storage costs. We clarify who approves extra costs and how they will be documented.

Instructions and document review

We compare invoices, transport documents and packing lists. Where consignee details, quantities, tariff headings or descriptions differ, we coordinate the review.

Clearance issues

We organize the response to a customs query, inspection or delay. We identify who can provide supporting evidence and which procedural step to consider.

What to prepare for the initial review

  • Importer or consignee details and Venezuelan tax ID
  • Brokerage proposal and available powers of attorney
  • Invoice, packing list and available transport document
  • Expected customs office, arrival date and any notices received
Legal referencesOrganic Customs Law · Official Gazette 6,507 Extraordinary

Frequently asked questions

Can you work with our existing customs broker?

Yes. We review the relevant authorization, appointment and division of responsibilities with the team already handling the shipment.

Who files the customs declaration?

The declaration is filed through the authorized broker or agency where required, subject to the Organic Customs Law and the exceptions for the applicable procedure.
