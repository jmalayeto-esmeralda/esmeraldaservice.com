Before shipment

The right paperwork starts with an accurate description of the goods

Product specifications, tariff classification, country of origin, intended use and transaction structure determine which customs duties, permits, technical standards and documentary controls may apply. There is no single checklist for every shipment.

Esmeralda coordinates the legal assessment and required documentation through its professional team. Acts reserved for licensed customs brokers or agencies are performed by duly authorized operators in accordance with Venezuelan law.

Frequently asked questions

What documents do I need to import into Venezuela?

Requirements depend on the goods and the operation. Commercial and shipping documents, customs declarations and product permits may be involved.

Is a customs broker required?

Venezuelan law reserves certain customs procedures to duly authorized customs brokers or agencies, subject to legal exceptions. Esmeralda coordinates legal advice and documentation.

Does every imported product need SENCAMER registration?

No universal answer applies. The specific tariff heading and technical regime must be reviewed.

Can my company import before I travel to Venezuela?

A properly documented representation arrangement may be possible, subject to corporate and customs requirements.

Can you estimate clearance duties and costs?

Yes, based on classification, value, origin, transaction details and the rates applicable at the relevant time.

What if cargo is held at customs?

We examine the legal or documentary grounds and the available administrative or legal response.
