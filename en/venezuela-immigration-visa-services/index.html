Types of matters

Three common routes, each with different requirements.

Tourist visa

Temporary visits and tourism

For people traveling for tourism, temporary visits or other activities that do not involve local remuneration or profit in Venezuela. The documentary route depends on nationality, place of residence and the relevant consular office.

Business visa

Business travel and commercial meetings

Designed for merchants, executives, company representatives and individuals who need to travel to Venezuela for commercial, corporate or financial activities. In practice, part of the process may be handled digitally through Cancillería Digital.

Investor visa

Investment and strategic presence

For individuals or company representatives who can evidence contacts and an investment accepted by the relevant Venezuelan authorities. Where the investment also requires a local entity, the immigration route should be coordinated with company formation and corporate services.

Practical notes

Requirements and timing are not identical in every case.

The applicable route may vary depending on nationality, country of residence, purpose of entry, existence of an inviting company, the proposed investment and the practice of the relevant consular office.

Official Venezuelan sources provide categories such as tourist visa, business transit visa and investor transit visa. In all cases, documentary requirements and consular fees should be confirmed at the time of filing with the relevant diplomatic or consular post.

Certain applications can involve online steps through Cancillería Digital. Processing and approval timelines depend on visa category, nationality, documentation and the responsible authority. Any case-specific estimate must be confirmed for the relevant application and does not guarantee approval.

Important: an investor visa does not automatically arise from the purchase of real estate. Viability depends on the nature of the investment and whether it fits the criteria accepted by the relevant Venezuelan authorities.

Official guidance is available from Venezuela’s Ministry of Foreign Affairs and its Cancillería Digital resources. Current requirements should be verified for each application.

Frequently asked questions

Common questions before starting the process.

Can every business trip be handled with a tourist visa?

Not necessarily. The correct category depends on the true purpose of the trip and the activities to be carried out in Venezuela. It is advisable to review the case before filing.

Is the business visa always handled in person?

Not always. Part of the process may begin or be managed digitally, but the precise route depends on the visa type, the consular office and the specific requirements applicable to the case.

Does buying real estate qualify someone for an investor visa?

Not by itself. The analysis should focus on whether the proposed investment fits the criteria required by the Venezuelan authorities for that immigration category.

Are consular fees included in the legal service?

As a general rule, consular fees and third-party costs are confirmed and quoted separately unless a specific engagement expressly states otherwise.

Can Esmeralda also coordinate the local corporate structure?

Yes. If the immigration matter is linked to an investment, a company or a market-entry project, we can coordinate the work with our Market Entry, Company Formation and Corporate Services practices.

Can several applications be prepared under one business logic?

Yes. Where a business group or family needs more than one immigration filing, a coordinated strategy may be structured to support documentary and operational consistency across the matter.
